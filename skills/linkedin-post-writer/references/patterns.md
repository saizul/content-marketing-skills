# LinkedIn hook, body and CTA patterns

## Hook formulas (first line)

| Formula | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Result + constraint | We cut production time by 40% without hiring. |
| Number + surprise | 3 of our best clients came from one old blog post. |
| Contrarian | Posting every day made our LinkedIn worse. |
| Mistake confession | I lost a $20k client because of one missing email. |
| Before / after | 6 months ago: 200 followers. Today: 9 inbound leads a month. |
| Specific question | How many of your blog posts get a second life after publishing? |
| Direct promise | The 20-minute brief that fixed our content calendar: |
| Observation | Every agency I talk to has the same content problem. |

Weak openers the script flags: "I'm excited/thrilled/humbled to announce", "Hello
everyone", "Happy Monday", "Just a quick post", "In today's fast-paced world",
"Have you ever wondered".

## Body structures

**Story** - hook / situation (when, where, who) / the turn / what you did / result /
lesson in one line / question.

**Listicle** - hook promising N items / "Here is what changed:" / numbered items, each a
bold claim on line 1 and one line of detail / summary line / question.

**Contrarian** - claim / what most people believe / why it breaks in practice (evidence) /
what to do instead / "Agree or disagree?" with a specific angle.

**How-to** - outcome / steps 1-5, each one line / the mistake to avoid / CTA to save or ask.

**Lesson learned** - mistake / the cost / what you changed / the rule you follow now.

**Case study** - client type and starting numbers / 3 moves / result with timeframe /
what made the difference / offer or question.

**Announcement done right** - lead with what the reader gets, not with your feelings:
"You can now turn one blog post into a week of LinkedIn posts in 5 minutes." Then how,
who it is for, and where to try it (first comment).

## No-emoji formatting

- Numbered lists `1.` `2.` and hyphen bullets `-` instead of emoji bullets
- Arrows (`->`) or a colon to point at results
- Short lines and blank lines carry the rhythm; capitals only for acronyms
- Do not use Unicode "bold" or "italic" letters: screen readers spell them out and they do
  not show up in search

## CTA patterns

| Goal | Close with |
| --- | --- |
| Comments | A specific question: "Which step would you cut first?" (not "Thoughts?") |
| DMs / leads | "If you want the template, comment 'brief' and I will send it." |
| Profile visits | "I write about content systems for small teams. Follow for the next one." |
| Clicks | Link in the first comment, mention it in the last line |
| Saves | "Save this for your next planning session." |

## Length and format

- Hard limit: 3,000 characters. Most strong posts are 600-1,500.
- One idea per post. If you need "also", it is two posts.
- Hashtags: 0-5, specific (`#ContentMarketing` over `#Business`), at the end.
- Tag people only when they are in the story and would want to be tagged.
