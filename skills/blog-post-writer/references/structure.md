# Post structures and hooks

## Pick the pattern from the search intent

| Intent signal in the keyword | Pattern | Must include |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "how to", "steps", "set up" | How-to | numbered steps, prerequisites, expected result, troubleshooting |
| "best", "ideas", "examples", "tips" | List | numbered H2s, one example each, a "how to choose" section |
| "x vs y", "alternatives", "or" | Comparison | verdict first, comparison table, "choose X if..., Y if..." |
| broad head term ("content marketing") | Pillar guide | definition, why it matters, process, links to every spoke |
| "template", "checklist", "example" | Asset page | the asset above the fold, how to use it, a filled-in example |
| brand + result ("how we...") | Case study | starting point, what you did, numbers, what you would change |

## How-to outline

```
# How to [do X] [qualifier: in 20 minutes / without Y]
Intro: the problem, the result, what the reader needs (3-5 sentences)
## What you need before you start
## Step 1: [verb + object]
## Step 2: ...
## Common mistakes (and how to fix them)
## Example: [real case]
## Next step (CTA)
```

## List outline

```
# [N] [Topic] [Benefit] ([Year] or [Audience])
Intro: who this is for and how the list was chosen
## 1. [Item]: [one-line why]
   what it is / when to use it / example / pitfall
...
## How to choose
## Summary table
```

## Comparison outline

```
# X vs Y: [Which is better for audience/use case]
Verdict in 2-3 sentences (who should pick which)
## Quick comparison (table: price, setup, strengths, limits)
## When X is the better choice
## When Y is the better choice
## What we use and why (first-hand)
## FAQ
```

## Case study outline

```
# How [client type] [achieved result] in [time]
The numbers up front (before / after)
## The starting point
## What we changed (3-5 moves, with screenshots)
## Results (with dates and the measurement source)
## What we would do differently
## How to apply this (CTA)
```

## Hooks

The first 2-3 sentences decide whether anyone reads on. Lead with one of:

| Hook | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Result first | "We publish twice a week with two people. Here is the calendar that makes it possible." |
| Problem the reader feels | "Your content calendar is not the problem. The missing brief is." |
| Surprising number (real) | "38% of the clicks came from posts older than a year." |
| Contrarian claim | "Posting every day hurt our traffic." |
| Direct answer (for question keywords) | "Publish 2-4 posts a month if you are a team of one. Here is why." |

Avoid: "In this article...", "In today's fast-paced world...", dictionary definitions,
throat-clearing about why the topic is important.

## Conversion

- One primary CTA that matches the stage: TOFU -> newsletter or template; MOFU -> lead
  magnet, webinar; BOFU -> demo, consultation, pricing.
- Place it after the first big win (mid-post) and at the end. Use a specific label
  ("Get the calendar template"), not "Click here".
