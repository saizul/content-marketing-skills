# Short video script (45-60 s, vertical)

| Time | Beat | Say (spoken, ~2.5 words per second) | On screen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3s | HOOK | [8 words max: result, question or bold claim] | [3-5 word caption] |
| 3-8s | SETUP | [the problem in one sentence] | [problem keyword] |
| 8-20s | POINT 1 | [claim + example] | [point title] |
| 20-32s | POINT 2 | [claim + example] | [point title] |
| 32-44s | POINT 3 | [claim + example] | [point title] |
| 44-55s | CTA | [one action: follow, comment a word, link in profile] | [CTA text] |

Rules: captions on (most people watch muted), cut every word that is not needed, show
the thing (screen recording, before/after) instead of describing it, one CTA.
B-roll ideas: [screenshots from the post, the template, a whiteboard sketch]
